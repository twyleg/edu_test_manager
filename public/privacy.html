Privacy & browser storage

This page describes the application’s behavior. The organization operating this deployment is responsible for its use of personal data, including student information. Contact that organization for its full privacy information, retention policies and data-protection contact. This technical notice does not replace the operator’s privacy policy.

What the app processes

Exam PDFs, student CSVs, names, points, marks, email addresses and shared attachments are processed in your browser. The app has no application backend. Local files are not automatically uploaded. Drive downloads, saving split PDFs or course results.json, renaming exams, creating Gmail drafts and sending selected drafts happen through the Google APIs when you request those actions. Copies saved to Drive or Gmail remain there until you manage or delete them.

Google services

Google sign-in and Picker scripts load only after you enable Google services in Privacy & storage settings. Google receives network information, including your IP address, and may access or set its own cookies. The header Connect Google button requests Drive, Gmail draft and Gmail label permissions together before enabling tasks. Connecting does not create drafts or send email; those actions require their task controls. Google may process data internationally under its terms and safeguards; see Google’s privacy policy. The app’s consent choice does not replace Google authorization or the operator’s responsibility for the data processing.

Browser storage

Item Purpose and duration
Privacy choice (local storage) Remembers your choice for 180 days; an expired choice is ignored and requested again.
Workflow selections and last student-list folder ID (local storage) Remembering is the default option when enabling Google. It starts only after choosing “Enable Google & remember folder”. Stored until you disable remembering, select local-only mode, or clear this site’s storage. Stores selected input, student-list, course and test Drive IDs and names, plus up to ten recent course folders and ten recent student lists. No CSV contents are stored.
Google access token (session storage) Reuses your Google session until the token expires; cleared on disconnect, local-only mode, or when the browser ends the session. Browser session restore may preserve session storage.

Matching and documentation share a temporary in-memory cache of exam PDFs and corner previews; documentation may prefetch the next exam. This cache is not written to browser storage and is cleared when changing course, disconnecting, selecting local-only mode or reloading. Documentation highlights store only field rectangle coordinates per course when remembering is enabled; these settings are removed when remembering is disabled or local-only mode is selected. No exam image or result values are included in those settings. The app itself does not set cookies or include advertising or analytics. Your browser may cache application files and local fonts. Hosting providers can receive connection information and keep access logs according to their policies. For GitHub Pages deployments, see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Your choices

You can use local PDF processing without Google or folder remembering. Open Privacy & storage settings at the bottom of the app to change your choice. Disabling remembering clears the saved folder ID and workflow selections. Local-only mode clears the app’s Google token and reloads the page to stop loaded integrations; it does not delete Google cookies or revoke permissions in your Google account. You can revoke the app’s permissions in Google account settings. Clearing website data also removes preferences. Finish current work before reloading.

Contact the deployment operator to exercise applicable data-protection rights, including access, correction, deletion and restriction. You may also contact the relevant supervisory authority. The operator must specify its identity, legal bases, recipients, retention and contact details for its deployment.
